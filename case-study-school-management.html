Case Study · Software and Web

A school management system built for how small Kenyan schools actually run

A single web app that replaces the spreadsheets, exercise books and paper registers most small schools run on. Built for a preparatory school in Kenya, and available for any school that wants the same.

School name withheld at the client's request. Screenshots below have the school's name and logo blurred for the same reason; student names shown are placeholder data, not real students.

"We used to do everything physically on paper, and it wasn't sustainable. Papers went missing, they piled up, and keeping track of everything became a real headache. Since switching to this system, my work and my teachers' work has become so much more efficient. Everything is in one place now, and nothing gets lost."

Catherine, Director

What it does

Everything a small school's admin runs on, in one place

No IT department, no installation, no training required beyond a login and password. It runs in a browser on whatever device is already in the office. Designed around the CBC curriculum (PP1 to Grade 6) and how Kenyan schools actually collect fees (M-Pesa, bank, cash) and communicate with parents (WhatsApp), not a generic system adapted after the fact.

Fee collection

Record M-Pesa, bank and cash payments against each student's real fee balance, with genuine typed-in payment references, not made-up codes. Automatically tracks who's paid, who's partial, and who owes what.

Unpaid fees & reminders

See defaulters at a glance and send a payment reminder straight to a parent's WhatsApp in one tap.

Attendance

Mark daily attendance per class, and print a register for a single day, a week, a month, or a whole term.

Timetable

An editable weekly timetable per class, built for how CBC schools actually structure their day, not a rigid template.

Lesson plans

Teachers log and file lesson plans by class and date.

CBC assessment & report cards

Scored on the official KNEC 8-level achievement scale (EE1–BE2), with report cards generated and archived per term so past reports never change.

Staff directory & payroll

A record of every staff member's role, contact details and salary, visible only to the people who should see it.

Finance overview

Real income and expenses for the term, not estimates.

Role-based access

The director, head teacher, class teachers and subject teachers each see only what's relevant to their role.

Your school's own branding

Your school's name, logo and contact details appear automatically on every receipt, invoice and report the system generates.
