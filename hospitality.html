What the booking agent does

Your front desk on WhatsApp, without the late nights

Answers guest questions 24/7

Rooms, rates, meals, check-in times, directions, activities, and policies, answered instantly at 2pm or 2am.

Takes the booking details

Collects dates, room type, number of guests, and contact details, so a booking request arrives complete.

Sends confirmations and payment details

Confirms the booking in the chat and shares your payment details, such as your M-Pesa Paybill or Till number and deposit terms.

Notifies you of every booking

You get each new booking and enquiry summary straight away, so nothing gets lost in a busy chat list.

Hands over to you when needed

Group bookings, complaints, or special requests are passed to you or your staff with the full conversation.

Works on WhatsApp and your website

The same agent can run on your WhatsApp Business number, as a chat on your website, or both.

Sunbird Lodge
Hi, how much is a double room for 2 nights from 12 December?9:41 PM
Hi! A double room is KES 9,000 per night with breakfast, so KES 18,000 for 2 nights. We have one free for 12 to 14 December. Would you like me to book it?9:41 PM
Yes please. Can I pay with M-Pesa?9:42 PM
Of course. To confirm, please pay a 50% deposit of KES 9,000 to Paybill 123456, account SUNBIRD. I have noted your booking and the lodge team will confirm once payment arrives.9:42 PM

What just happened

  • Guest messaged at 9:41 PM, after the front desk closed
  • The agent answered the price question and checked the dates
  • It took the booking and shared the M-Pesa deposit details
  • The lodge was notified with the full booking
